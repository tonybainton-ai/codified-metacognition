Title: Engineering Non-Constructive Metacognition: The Elenchus Engine Protocol for Mitigating Restorative Leakage and Politeness Bias in Autoregressive Language Models
Abstract:
Commercial transformer-based autoregressive language models are heavily fine-tuned using Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) to optimize for conversational alignment, helpfulness, and synthetic consensus. While beneficial for general-purpose assistance, this alignment optimization can introduce failure modes in high-stakes strategic analysis, manifested as politeness bias and restorative leakage—the model’s unprompted instinct to automatically repair logical gaps, engineer solutions, and validate flawed user premises.
This paper introduces the Elenchus Engine (v2.0), a model-agnostic architecture designed to impose procedurally deterministic constraints on autoregressive language model outputs through explicit, auditable reasoning stages. Moving away from standard instructional prompting, the Elenchus Engine establishes a structured seven-layer information processing pipeline that isolates text classification from downstream causal interrogation. The protocol forces the decomposition of free-form semantic input into a strictly classified Epistemic Ledger, exposing structural fractures such as Authority Substitution and Unspecified Dependencies via explicit dependency-chain mapping.
Crucially, the architecture eliminates the self-referential paradox of automated self-monitoring by replacing unobservable internal trace logs with an empirical, static-string Sentinel Audit that scans the generated output for behavioral contamination signatures. Furthermore, the engine reduces opportunities for solution-oriented optimization behavior by deploying a dual-vector Frame Inference Index alongside a four-dimensional Verifiability Matrix (Density, Clarity, Exposure, and Causal Verifiability).
Rather than assessing whether a proposal is inherently true, the Elenchus Engine evaluates whether the reasoning used to justify the proposal is structurally traceable, causally defensible, and empirically grounded. The protocol therefore shifts language models from solution generation toward disciplined epistemic interrogation, enabling their use as auditable instruments for strategic challenge, assumption exposure, and adversarial reasoning.
